The assassination of the 4 main [[deity of concepts]] took place at their meeting rooms just after [[VIDO]] was released by [[ETERON]]. They would all raised their concerns with the unsealing of VIDO for 4 minutes and 25 seconds. In the last 25 seconds of the meeting, [[CHRONOBOWUS]] and the deity of concept Power would begin fighting on what to do about the situation to the point where space and time surrounding the dimension would break and glitch out, in which they would get assassinated by an unidentified individual.
