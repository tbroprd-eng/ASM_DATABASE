虛空風格 also known as Void fighting style, is a technique that almost any type of Sapient with sentience can do, and was early most seen by mortal beings in China due to their mythology coincidentally showing on how to unlock an ability similar to the fighting style, and was later banned in use due to how deadly it was to who got hit by the attack. It was later re discovered by the soviets in the 1950s and was used for The 13th Department for decades until most of the people who used the void fighting style slowly developed [[VIS]] and later died because of it.

### History
During the reign of [[VIDO]]  when he was in power during the [[heavenly war]], he would use 90% of his power to alter reality so almost every single being, immortal or mortal, has the genetic code where if unlocked, they would become a vessel or puppet to VIDO. This would be used to eliminate mass amounts of [[xetrixes]] and high ranking elites during the war by making them kill themselves.
