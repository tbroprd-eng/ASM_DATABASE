Hologramicos is method or practice that involves embedding letters, and languages into a specific angle of light, acting as a way of encrypting documents other than carvings . It is also considered a document of both the letters and the light, since it combines these 2 things together. Hologramicos are recognized as one of the strongest forms of physical encryption, taking about 2-3 weeks to fully decrypt due to its nature of needing it to be manually be decrypted by brute forcing light positions, emissions, direction, color, quantity, and all other aspects that will fill this entire document.

### Origin
Hologramicos were invented by an unknown group of light manipulating deities 50 billion years before the [[heavenly war]], when tensions between the [[VOT]] and the Society grew. 
By time, the practice of Hologramicos spread all across reality to the masses as a way of documenting and encryption other than storing it in their mind.
This would spread a spike in ways that no deity can see. From Classified plans that would betray one another, to secret operations that violated certain laws, leading radical factions to form across the society.
