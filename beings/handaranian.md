#CLASSIFIED 
Handaranian, or called with their scientific name Manus Sentire are humanoid aliens who usually reside at Bx71. They are mostly known to be a very similar species to Humans, and also have 58 countries on their home world and 122 more on 100 different planets.
The Handaranians are classified as a Type V civilization, being the 25th species to use multiple universes to harness energy.
### Appearance
Their physical appearance is bipedal humanoids who have Malibu-like skin color. Also having an extra limb on their top right of their head, with a set of 3-7 unique yellow to orange stripes on their extra limb, Handaranians also have spikes on their back, shoulder to upper arm 

### Abilities
Handaranians have the abilities pyrokinesis and telekinesis; this is from the magic properties of the minerals of their planet. They can control these
