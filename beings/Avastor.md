### About 
Name: Avastor Apollyono
Threat Level: Complex multiversal
Classification: endangerment to reality
Known Aliases: Avastor
Origin: BX-345 Universe 99IOC
Nature of Hazard: Dark magic
Core Objective: Eliminate Division HTY's core member [[Xerxion]](Originally the universe version of it)
Status: Eliminated by [[ETERON]]

Avastor is a branch species of [[handaranian]] called Hadrenians, who look the same, just with hair and no abilities in their disposal, and is classified as a threat and a direct endangerment to reality due to his dark magic and sheer willpower to obliterate anything that gets in his way, including Deities of concepts

### Reasons for revenge
His revenge for [[Xerxion]](Specifically, it's universe counterpart) is because his master was killed, along with the planet being burned alive by [[the Grabber]] in disguise as Xerxion. This would lead Avastor to train by himself for 10 years, where he would track down that individual.

### Record of deities and important people that he killed(Based on what the Xerxion in that universe said, this is confirmed)
Avastor has killed numerous individuals, such as their division-HTY member Python, who was impaled and shortly erased by a beam of dark magic, [[Terpy]], who was assassinated at night, and the 4 main [[deity of concepts]].

He was able to kill most of the deities in this order in about 5 minutes:

Power- Head and consciousness removed, resulting on him demanifesting

Life- Gave her living dark matter that grew on her body that eventually exploded

Death- Beats him up with his fists and scythe to death

Judgement- Lets him absorb dark magic, then reverses that action, leading Judgement to be sucked into Avastor's body as power.

Time- 1 shots him with his scythe, but time later reverses everything before the fight, subduing him before anything could go wrong


# Abilities
Avastor is able to manipulate almost anything his hands touch, including other abilities and magic, just with negative and dark energy. This gives him the ability to have the strength of Gods in his hands. He can also rewind any object in time for about 1 minute maximum with his dark manipulation magic; this includes abilities, age, life, death, and any concept manipulation.

Along with these abilities, he can control his movement 50 times faster than the speed of light(Can see in 0.50x) whilst being unaffected by any damage whatsoever for only about 5 seconds. When this runs out, his strength and abilities would increase 10-fold for 3 hits with infinite stamina. If he kills an individual with one of the 3 attacks, his unreal speed would activate again. This would repeat until he does kill anyone with one of his 3 attacks.

### His Dominion
Avastor's Dominion is a sentient scythe that was enchanted to temporarily steal a person's ability and use it as its own for about 20 seconds, doubling its power on the scythe.

Originally, his Dominion was an axe that evolved into a scythe from the leftover souls and abilities inside him; this also made it stronger to the point where it could defeat Gods and other similar deities.
