### About 
Name: Avastor Apollyono
Threat Level: Complex multiversal
Classification: endangerment to reality
Known Aliases: Avastor
Origin: BX-345 Universe 99IOC
Nature of Hazard: Dark magic
Core Objective: Eliminate Division HTY's core member [[Xerxion]](Originally the universe version of it)
Status: Eliminated by [[ETERON]]

Avastor is a classified threat and a direct endangerment to reality due to his dark magic and sheer willpower to obliterate anything that gets in his way, including Deities of concepts.

# Abilities
Avastor is able to manipulate almost anything his hands touch, including other abilities and magic, just with negative and dark energy. This gives him the ability to have the strength of Gods in his hands. He can also rewind any object in time for about 1 minute maximum, with his dark manipulation magic
